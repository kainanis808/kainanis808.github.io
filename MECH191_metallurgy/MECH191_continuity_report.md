# MECH 191 Site Continuity Check

Prepared August 20, 2026. This checks link integrity and topic alignment across the course homepage, the reference/visualization HTML pages, the weekly slide decks and note handouts, the semester lab Modules document, and the syllabus.

## What I checked

I crawled every internal link on all 66 HTML pages (root, referencePages, Visualizations, slideDecks, semesterActivity) and confirmed each one resolves to a real file, checked for pages nothing links to, compared every week's slide-deck title against its note-handout PDF (title text and page count, all 15 present weeks), and lined up the week-by-week topics in unitsTopics.html and the syllabus against the Modules.docx and Portfolio Answer Key module headers.

## Issues found

**Homepage title tag says "Mechatronics," not "MECH."** In `index.html`, the `<title>` tag reads "Mechatronics 191: Metallurgy," while the visible `<h1>` on the same page reads "MECH 191: Metallurgy," and every other page on the site (unitsTopics.html, and the four section index pages) titles itself "MECH191" consistently. Since the `<title>` tag is what shows up in the browser tab and in search results, this is the one place a visitor would see the course referred to by a different name than everywhere else. Worth a one-word fix.

**The Week 1 "Intro" deck is orphaned.** `slideDecks/pdf/MECH191_Week1_Intro.pdf`, its matching `.pptx`, and its thumbnail all exist in the folder, but nothing on the site links to them — `slideDecks/index.html` only shows the Week 1 "Atomic Structure & Bonding" deck. The syllabus lists Week 1's topics as "Syllabus, PPE" *and* "Unit 1 – Foundations," which lines up neatly with there being two decks that week — a short orientation deck (Syllabus/PPE) plus the content lecture. Right now the orientation deck is unreachable from the site; it's likely just missing its card on the slide decks page.

**Three navigation links on the homepage use inconsistent or fragile paths.** All the other links in `index.html`'s nav list use clean relative paths (`./referencePages/index.html`, `./Visualizations/`, etc.), but three don't follow that pattern: "About the Instructor" points to `../genericPages/about.html`, which sits outside this folder, so I wasn't able to confirm it resolves — that's worth a manual click-check since I only have visibility into the MECH191_metallurgy folder itself. "Units and Topics" points to `../MECH191_metallurgy/unitsTopics.html` — a round-trip path (up one level, then back into the same folder by name) that happens to still land on the right file today, but only because it depends on this folder always being named exactly `MECH191_metallurgy` in the parent site; a plain `unitsTopics.html` would be more robust and match the style of every other link on the page. And at the bottom of the page, "Back to Main Course Page" links to `/MECH191_metallurgy/index.html` — which is this exact page. As written, that "back" link just reloads itself instead of returning to whatever the actual top-level course/portfolio page is supposed to be.

**Modules 9 and 10 don't explain why their lab topic differs from that week's lecture.** Starting with Module 12 (Week 13), every module in `MECH191_Modules.docx` whose lab topic diverges from that week's lecture opens with a clear transition sentence — for example, Module 13 begins "Welding process fundamentals are covered in this week's lecture material; this module's hands-on specimen instead examines how zinc content changes brass microstructure..." That pattern also applies to Weeks 10 and 11: per `slideDecks/index.html` (and the syllabus's Unit 4 schedule), Week 10's lecture is "Casting Processes & Defect Recognition" and Week 11's is "Metal Forming Processes," but Module 9 (Week 10) and Module 10 (Week 11) jump straight into aluminum-alloy content ("Wrought Aluminum Alloy Systems" and "Aluminum Alloys: Heat-Treatable vs. Weldable Structural Grades") with no such framing sentence. A student who's used to the later modules explaining the lecture/lab split would likely read Modules 9 and 10 as mismatched or out of order, when the underlying design (lab runs a separate specimen-revisit thread) is actually the same one the doc explains everywhere else. Adding a matching opening sentence to those two modules would close that gap.

**Minor formatting mismatch between the Modules doc and its Answer Key.** Module headers in `MECH191_Modules.docx` are styled "MODULE 1 · WEEK 1 | Atomic Structure & Bonding" (pipe separator), while `MECH191_Portfolio_AnswerKey.docx` uses "MODULE 1 · WEEK 1 — Atomic Structure & Bonding" (em dash, plus a specimen list). The content lines up perfectly module-for-module; only the separator punctuation differs. Cosmetic, but easy to make consistent since these two documents are meant to be read side by side.
