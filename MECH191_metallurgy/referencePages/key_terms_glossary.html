Key Terms & Vocabulary Guide

Terms to notate during lecture and research further — organized by unit and week

This guide follows the course roadmap unit by unit. For every week, it lists the vocabulary worth writing down during lecture and looking up further if the in-class explanation leaves you wanting more depth, plus a short working definition for context. It also includes a few standalone reference sections — units of measurement and dimensional analysis, a quick math skills review, a geometry and right-triangle trigonometry refresher, and a collected list of the engineering equations used across the semester — since those skills come up throughout the course rather than in just one week.
