About this list

Entries here are stored only in this browser’s local storage on this device — nothing is uploaded anywhere, and nothing is shared automatically with students or other devices. If you clear your browser data you’ll lose it, so use Export as JSON to back up your list (or move it to another computer with Import JSON). Once you’re happy with the links, send the exported JSON back and it can be baked into a permanent, non-editable version of this page for students.

Good places to look: the University of Cambridge DoITPoMS Micrograph Library (CC BY-NC-SA 2.0 UK) and its Flickr mirror, and the Copper Development Association’s microstructure reference pages for the brass/copper specimens. Automated tools have had trouble reliably reaching these sites (frequent 403s, and some pages that fail in a real browser even when a fetch tool reports success) — browsing them directly yourself is the most reliable way to find a working link.
