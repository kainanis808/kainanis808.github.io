Semester Alloy Research Portfolio

MECH191: Metallurgy — the 15-module research portfolio completed across the semester

This is the module document referenced throughout the course: one module per lecture week, each assigning one or more alloys from the course specimen set for you to research using real, published composition, properties, processing, and applications data. Each module includes research & documentation, short-answer questions, and critical-thinking questions applying that research to the week's engineering concepts — no microscope work or lab session is required to complete it. All 15 completed modules, including a running Hardness Log built up across the semester, are compiled and submitted together as a single portfolio at the end of the semester.
