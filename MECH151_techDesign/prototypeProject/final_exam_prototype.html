Final Exam — Week 17

Prototype Project

A team-built physical prototype, presented to the class

MECH151 closes with a hands-on final exam: a physical prototype that your team designs, documents, builds, and presents. It draws on skills built across all three units — sketching and modeling, detailed documentation, assemblies and joining methods, and manufacturing-ready files and shop processes — and it is graded separately from your Design Portfolio.

Goal: Demonstrate that you can carry a design from concept through a finished, physically working prototype — choosing materials and a joining method deliberately, documenting the part the way a manufacturer would need it, and presenting and defending the result.

Course learning objectives this exam assesses

The prototype is where the last two course learning objectives are demonstrated, and it relies on the first three, which you have practiced through Units 1 and 2:

  1. Demonstrate basic part modeling using 3D CAD software — every component of your prototype starts as a parametric part model.
  2. Demonstrate basic assembly modeling using 3D CAD software — your parts are mated into an assembly before anything is built.
  3. Simulate a complete assembly model using components — check fit, motion, and your joining method in CAD so problems are caught before they cost material.
  4. Produce a component utilizing CAM software — at least one part is taken from model to manufacturing-ready file (toolpath, sliced print, or cut file) and made on shop equipment.
  5. Produce a prototype from multiple substrates — the finished prototype integrates at least 2 different bulk material types, joined by at least 1 joining method.

Team structure

You may complete the Final Exam individually or in a team of up to 3 students. Teams design, build, and present together.

What your team submits

  • Prototype design proposal — team members, what design is being made, and how it will integrate at least 2 different types of bulk material (e.g., thermoplastic + raw/processed stock) and demonstrate at least 1 joining method between 2 parts (e.g., heat insert, snap fit)
  • Manufacturing documentation — all pertinent drawings, with the views and GD&T callouts needed to build the part
  • Work breakdown summary — who on the team did what
  • Self reflection — completed individually by each team member
  • Presentation — your team presents and defends its design to the class during the Final Exam

When and where

Logistics: The Final Exam is held at CE405 on Wednesday, December 16, 2026, 4:00–6:30 PM. Teams present their design to the class during this window. Exams must be submitted to the instructor by 6:30 PM to be considered for grading — work submitted after the end time automatically scores zero.

Bring to the exam: your finished prototype, your manufacturing documentation (drawings with pertinent views and GD&T callouts), your team’s Work Breakdown Summary, and each member’s Self Reflection. Your Prototype Design Proposal — including any revisions from the Week 11 design review — is part of the evaluation as well.

What you'll produce: a team prototype design proposal, correct manufacturing-ready files and GD&T documentation for at least one process, a finished physical prototype built with shop equipment you've been checked out on, a work breakdown summary, and a self reflection.

Worth 30% of your course grade (Prototype + Final Exam, combined). The Design Portfolio (50% of your course grade) is a separate submission, emailed directly to the instructor or sent via UH FileDrop — see the Semester Design Portfolio page. For full grading details, see the syllabus.
