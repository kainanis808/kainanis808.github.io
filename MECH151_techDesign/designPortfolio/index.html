The template is a starting point, not a checklist

You are responsible for the content of your portfolio. The template gives you the structure and tells you what each unit is evaluated on — it does not tell you what to design. The answer boxes are sized to keep the document compact, not to cap what you write. For every unit you must present a design of your own choosing and include whatever CAD models, drawings, screenshots, measured properties, and written reasoning are needed to demonstrate proficiency in that unit's topics. “Your Design” and “Designer Feedback” are open-ended: expand any section or add rows as far as you need. A portfolio that only fills in the blanks will not show proficiency.

Submitting your portfolio

Your completed portfolio — all four units together — is submitted as a single package through Lamaku (the course LMS) and is worth 50% of your course grade, due by the date announced in class and on the syllabus.

File formats to include

  • Native CAD file(s) in your software's format (e.g. .sldprt, .sldasm, .slddrw) so your full parametric feature tree is preserved and can be graded.
  • A neutral STEP copy (.step / .stp) of each part and assembly.
  • A PDF of every drawing sheet (multiview, detail, and assembly drawings), in addition to the native drawing file.

File naming

  • Use one consistent pattern for every file: LastName_FirstName_UnitX_ShortPartName.ext — e.g. Santos_Kai_Unit1_BracketPart.sldprt.
  • Keep names short; use underscores instead of spaces or special characters.

Folder structure

  • One folder per unit: Unit1_CADFundamentals, Unit2_CADAdvanced, Unit3_DFA, Unit4_DFM — each holding that unit's native files, STEP exports, and drawing PDFs.
  • Keep an assembly and every part file it references together in the same folder so the assembly opens without missing references.

How and when to submit

  • Compress your complete portfolio folder (all four units) into a single .zip file.
  • Upload the .zip through Lamaku as your Design Portfolio submission by the announced due date.
  • Before submitting, confirm your .zip opens cleanly and every file is present — corrupted or incomplete uploads cannot be graded.
