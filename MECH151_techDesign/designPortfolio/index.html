Semester Design Portfolio

MECH 151: Technical Design — Prototyping · the template completed across the semester, one module per instructional week

This is the portfolio template referenced throughout the course. It tracks your design work across all three units — CAD Fundamentals, Design for Assembly, and Design for Manufacturing. Each unit follows the same three-part structure: What’s Evaluated (the skills and deliverables graded for that unit), Your Design (an open-ended section where you present a design of your own that demonstrates proficiency in that unit’s topics), and Designer Feedback (a running log of feedback received and how you plan to address it). All three completed units are compiled and submitted together as a single portfolio package.

Working in Google Docs (optional)

Prefer Google Docs to Microsoft Word? Upload the Word template to Google Drive and open it as a Google Doc. The conversion keeps the template’s layout — headings, tables, and answer boxes — so you can work on it from any device.

Upload the template to Google Drive

  1. Download the Word version (.docx) above.
  2. Go to drive.google.com (opens in a new tab) and sign in with your UH Google account (your @hawaii.edu address).
  3. Click + New → File upload, then select MECH151_Semester_Design_Portfolio_Template.docx. (You can also drag the file into the Drive window.)

Open it as a Google Doc

  1. In Drive, right-click the uploaded file → Open with → Google Docs. (Or double-click it to preview, then choose Open with Google Docs at the top of the screen.)
  2. The file opens with a .DOCX label next to its title — it is still a Word file at this point. Go to File → Save as Google Docs to convert it.
  3. A new Google Doc copy opens in a new tab. Do all of your work in this Google Doc copy; you can delete the uploaded .docx from Drive afterward if you’d like.
  4. Rename the copy so it’s easy to find — e.g. LastName_FirstName_MECH151_Portfolio.

Check the formatting and submit

  • Scroll through the converted document once and compare it against the PDF version. If any table or answer box shifted, fix it before you start filling things in.
  • When you’re ready to submit, either share the Google Doc (or a Drive folder containing it) as described in the next section, or use File → Download → Microsoft Word (.docx) or PDF Document (.pdf) and include the downloaded file in your portfolio .zip (see Submitting your portfolio below).

Tip: to have Drive convert Word files automatically when you upload them, open Drive’s Settings (gear icon) and turn on Convert uploads.

Sharing your portfolio from Google Drive

Instead of emailing a .zip, you can share your portfolio straight from Google Drive — either the Google Doc on its own, or a Drive folder that holds the Google Doc together with all of your CAD files. Sharing the folder is recommended, since it keeps everything for your submission in one place.

Option A — Share just the document

  1. Open your portfolio Google Doc and click the blue Share button (top right).
  2. Under Add people, groups, and calendar events, type kainanis@hawaii.edu.
  3. Set the role to Commenter so the instructor can leave feedback directly in your document.
  4. Leave Notify people checked, add a short message (e.g. “MECH151 Design Portfolio – LastName, FirstName”), and click Send.

Option B — Share a folder with the document and CAD files

  1. In Drive, click + New → New folder and name it LastName_FirstName_MECH151_Portfolio.
  2. Move your portfolio Google Doc into the folder: right-click the Doc → Organize → Move, then choose the new folder. (You can also drag it in.)
  3. Open the folder and upload your CAD work with + New → Folder upload. Upload each unit folder (Unit1_CADFundamentals, Unit2_DFA, Unit3_DFM) so the same structure and file names described under Submitting your portfolio are kept. CAD files are stored exactly as uploaded — Drive does not convert them.
  4. Right-click the portfolio folder → Share → Share, add kainanis@hawaii.edu, set the role to Commenter, and click Send. Everything inside the folder — including files you add later — is shared automatically.

Before you send

  • Make sure you’re signed in with your @hawaii.edu Google account, and leave General access set to Restricted — only people you add can open it.
  • Open the folder and confirm every file finished uploading (no files still in progress or showing an error).
  • The shared document or folder is your submission — share it so it is received by the end of the final exam period (Wednesday, December 16, 2026, 6:30 PM).

The template is a starting point, not a checklist

You are responsible for the content of your portfolio. The template gives you the structure and tells you what each unit is evaluated on — it does not tell you what to design. The answer boxes are sized to keep the document compact, not to cap what you write. For every unit you must present a design of your own choosing and include whatever CAD models, drawings, screenshots, measured properties, and written reasoning are needed to demonstrate proficiency in that unit’s topics. “Your Design” and “Designer Feedback” are open-ended: expand any section or add rows as far as you need. A portfolio that only fills in the blanks will not show proficiency.

Submitting your portfolio

Your completed portfolio — all three units together — is submitted directly to the instructor by email and is worth 50% of your course grade. It must be submitted and received by the end of the final exam period (Wednesday, December 16, 2026, 6:30 PM) to be graded.

File formats to include

  • Native CAD file(s) in your software’s format (e.g. .sldprt, .sldasm, .slddrw) so your full parametric feature tree is preserved and can be graded.
  • A neutral STEP copy (.step / .stp) of each part and assembly.
  • A PDF of every drawing sheet (multiview drawings), in addition to the native drawing file.

File naming

  • Use one consistent pattern for every file: LastName_FirstName_UnitX_ShortPartName.ext — e.g. Santos_Kai_Unit1_BracketPart.sldprt.
  • Keep names short; use underscores instead of spaces or special characters.

Folder structure

  • One folder per unit: Unit1_CADFundamentals, Unit2_DFA, Unit3_DFM — each holding that unit’s native files, STEP exports, and drawing PDFs.
  • Keep an assembly and every part file it references together in the same folder so the assembly opens without missing references.

How and when to submit

  • Compress your complete portfolio folder (all three units) into a single .zip file.
  • Email the .zip to kainanis@hawaii.edu as your Design Portfolio submission — either as a direct attachment, or through the UH FileDrop service (recommended, since a full portfolio .zip is usually too large for email) addressed to the same email — or share your portfolio from Google Drive (see Sharing your portfolio from Google Drive above) — so that it is received by the end of the final exam period (Wednesday, December 16, 2026, 6:30 PM). Portfolios received after that time will not be graded.
  • Before submitting, confirm your .zip opens cleanly and every file is present — corrupted or incomplete uploads cannot be graded.
