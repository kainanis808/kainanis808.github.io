MECH151 Unit Reference Pages

Plain-language overviews of each course unit for MECH151: Technical Design – Prototyping — what the unit covers, why it matters, and what you'll produce.

Unit 1 — Weeks 4–7

  • CAD Fundamentals (Basic) Sketching and constraints, orthographic projection and multiview theory, descriptive geometry, primary and secondary views, dimensioning standards, and the first parametric part model.

Unit 2 — Weeks 7–10

  • CAD Advanced Section views, auxiliary views, thread and fastener representation, production and detail drawings, an introduction to tolerancing, CAD configuration, and CAD/CAM data translation.

Unit 3 — Weeks 11–13

  • Design for Assembly (DFA) Assembly modeling with mates and constraints, building assemblies from part models, fasteners in assemblies, exploded assembly drawings and BOMs, and DFA design principles.

Unit 4 — Weeks 14–16

  • Design for Manufacturing (DFM) Manufacturing-ready files, additive and subtractive processes, laser cutting, DFM design considerations, machine safety and operation, and the final prototype project.
